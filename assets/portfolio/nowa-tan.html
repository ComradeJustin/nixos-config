About Nowa-Tan And How She Was Made

What is she?

She is an AI VTuber which utilize my own custom setup in terms of her training and behaviour.

So why did she exist? And what was her origin

Originally she was made to be a discord bot meant to clone a member of a discord server for fun. At the same time, I was utilizing and testing out experimental neural-network architectures for rhythm games, namely Osu! and Quaver. I found that Quaver was kind of hard to train, so I ended deciding to do Osu!

Which is what led every to immedietly and instantly scream "THIS IS LITERALLY NEURO-SAMA", even though I didn't even have a VTuber. 1

So I just converted her from a discord based LLM to a VTuber as per literally the whole universe yelling at me to do so, so thats why she exists now.

What is the custom architecture and setup?

Well her speaking part is an LLM, I think most people in 2026 are plenty familiar with the parts. Though, what makes her interesting is that she does actually have reasoning, and I trained her thinking and speaking to be kind of similar. So she actually sometimes think out loud which is kind of funny. 2

In terms of her architecture, shes just an transformer. But what I would instead like to highlight the focus on is the Osu module. Where it is decently cool and novel, rather then using transformers and just scaling up the model and data, I actually implement interesting concepts in her Osu module. Specifically, I use a closed-form continuous-time neural network, and train the vision using JEPA. Which in simple terms is she approximates more realistic neural dynamics, and she actually learns without memorizing pixel by pixel but rather abstractions/concepts of whats happening on the screen (JEPA).

Her LLM module also uses a custom training setup, I do a custom two step process. Where I first bake her understanding of how to actually talk to twitch chat and multiple users (LLMs are typically built to respond to one user at a time), how to actually control her intergrations etc. The cool part is that she can actually skip turns purposely, so she can actually choose to ignore you.

Then the second stage trains her how to actually think like herself, and speak like her self, this is where she gains her personality and behaviour. And where her actual data lies, since stage 1 is just a bunch of administrative and code BS. 1
